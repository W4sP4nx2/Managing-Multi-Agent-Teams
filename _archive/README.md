# Archived Course Materials

This folder holds older or replaced course notebooks, tests, exercises, and technical references that are no longer on the learner Golden Path. They are retained for reference and are not required to complete the course.

The current learner sequence is the seven notebooks listed in the [root notebook manifest](../notebook_manifest.yml). Supporting comparison notebooks live in [`../extensions/`](../extensions/README.md). Advanced cross-organization A2A materials remain under [`advanced_scaling/`](advanced_scaling/README.md). That archive also retains the [legacy FastAPI Capstone implementation](advanced_scaling/assignments/starter_code/ex5_capstone_solution.py) and [A2A governance starters and solutions](advanced_scaling/assignments/starter_code/). The [archived reference architecture](technical_implementation_guide.md) and legacy [test suite](tests/README.md) are archived here rather than mixed into the learner path.

The four retired [slide supplements](slides/supplemental/README.md) are retained as historical references. The learner route uses one main presentation.

Deck versions v13, v14, and v15 are also retained under `slides/` here. The learner route uses the [six-slide main deck](../slides/managing_multi_agent_teams_6_slides.pptx); the root `slides/` folder also retains v16 as a longer historical reference.

The [seven-agent CrewAI research project](crewai_learning/README.md) and its former six-week/Homework G materials are historical. The active [three-agent advanced reference](../crewai_learning/README.md) demonstrates enforced task contracts and bounded escalation.

[Archived long-form assignments](assignments/README.md) retain the superseded prompts, starter files, and solution guides. [Earlier offline curriculum pages](curriculum_offline/) preserve the prior navigation and delivery framing.
